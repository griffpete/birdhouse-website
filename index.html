Training details

Most groomers are too booked to slow down.

A rushed groom shows up on the dog: tense body language, uneven cuts, razor burn. Birdhouse books fewer dogs a day on purpose. That leaves time for breaks, careful handling around sensitive spots, and a grooming experience built around your dog's comfort.
