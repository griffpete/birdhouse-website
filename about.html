Founder & dog care professional

Trainer first, then groomer

I spent two years as a professional dog trainer before I moved into grooming. That order matters. A trainer learns to read a dog before anything goes wrong: the stiff tail, the held breath, the paw that pulls back a half-second early. On the grooming table, that is the difference between a dog who gets through the cut and a dog who leaves calmer than they arrived.

I've completed a full grooming apprenticeship and continued my education at SUPERZOO, where I took a class with Nathan and Kathlena Austin. Both have earned AKC Master Groomer of the Year honors. I'm also pet CPR certified. Those experiences shape the way we care for dogs at Birdhouse: thoughtful handling, clear communication, and attention to each dog's comfort. That's the standard I want Birdhouse to grow around.

Why Birdhouse books fewer dogs

A lot of groomers are overbooked and have to move fast, and it shows up on the dog: anxious body language, uneven cuts, razor burn. At Birdhouse, we leave room in the schedule for careful grooming, breaks, and each dog's individual needs.

The same goes for training. We make time to understand how each dog settles, what sets them off, and what they're working on. That individual attention is at the heart of Birdhouse.

Questions about Dani & Birdhouse

Who owns Birdhouse Dog Co. in Ivins, Utah?

Dani Bird is the founder and owner of Birdhouse Dog Co., a dog grooming and training business in Ivins, Utah, serving the St. George area and Southern Utah. She founded Birdhouse with a focus on thoughtful grooming, practical training, and individual care.

What dog training and grooming experience does Dani have?

Dani worked as a professional dog trainer for two years before moving into grooming. She has completed a full grooming apprenticeship and attended a class at SUPERZOO with Nathan and Kathlena Austin, both AKC Master Groomer of the Year winners. She is also pet CPR certified. Her training background helps her recognize stress and adapt her handling during grooming.

How does Birdhouse keep care personal?

Birdhouse focuses on your dog’s coat, comfort, and training goals. Share past experiences, preferences, and handling concerns when you book so we can plan care around your dog and keep you involved along the way.

How does Dani’s training background influence grooming?

Dani’s training background shaped Birdhouse’s approach to grooming: watch for signs of stress, adjust the pace, and leave time for careful handling and breaks. If your dog has had a difficult experience, tell us before booking so we can discuss an appropriate first visit.

How can I find out which service my dog needs?

Text or call us at (916) 513-5280. For grooming, send a coat photo, breed, and weight; for training, describe what is happening and the goals you have for your dog. You can also compare the grooming packages or request a training consult.
