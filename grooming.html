Nervous dogs welcome

Dani trained dogs professionally for two years before she picked up clippers, so she reads stress early: a stiff tail, a held breath, a paw pulled back. She adjusts instead of pushing through.

If your dog has had a bad groom before, say so when you book. Sometimes the first appointment is a short, low-pressure visit that ends before anything hard happens. That is a win, and it is how the next one gets easier.

Dog grooming questions

Do you offer one-on-one dog grooming near St. George?

Yes. Birdhouse Dog Co. offers one-on-one grooming at its Ivins studio, at 740 S Red Mountain Blvd #21, near St. George. Appointments allow time for individual attention, breaks, and careful handling.

How much does dog grooming cost at Birdhouse Dog Co.?

Grooming prices are based on your dog’s weight: full grooms are $79–$129, bath and trim appointments are $49–$69, and bath and brush appointments are $39–$59. Matting, heavy shedding, and extra handling time can affect the final quote. See the package prices by weight; We confirm the quote when we meet your dog.

What is included in a full groom?

A full groom includes a bath, blow dry, full brush out, full body haircut in a breed-standard or requested style, nail trim, ear cleaning, and gland expression. Birdhouse Dog Co. offers full grooms at the Ivins studio for dogs whose coats need a haircut.

Should I book a bath and trim or a bath and brush?

A bath and trim includes a bath, blow dry, brush out, trimming between the eyes, paw pads and sanitary trim, nail trim, and ear cleaning. A bath and brush includes a bath, blow dry, brush out, nail trim, and ear cleaning, without a haircut. Send us a coat photo if you are unsure which package fits your dog.

Do you groom doodles, poodles, and other breeds?

Yes. Birdhouse offers grooming for doodles, poodles, spaniels, shih tzus, and many other dogs. Coat type and condition help determine the right service. Send a photo and your dog’s breed and weight so we can confirm the appointment type and price.

Can you groom a nervous dog or a dog with a difficult grooming history?

Birdhouse Dog Co. welcomes nervous dogs. Dani’s professional dog training background shaped an approach that pays attention to stress and adjusts handling during grooming. Tell us about past experiences before booking. We may recommend a shorter first visit or handling practice to help your dog become more comfortable.

How long should I allow for a grooming appointment in Ivins?

Plan for a few hours rather than a fixed appointment length. Grooming time depends on your dog’s coat, size, and comfort with handling. We work at your dog’s pace, take breaks as needed, and text you when your dog is ready for pickup.

What should I send before my dog’s first grooming visit?

Send your dog’s breed, weight, coat condition, vaccine records, and any concerns about handling or previous grooming experiences. A coat photo helps us recommend a package. Vaccines are required for each appointment. You can include these details in your grooming request or text us at (916) 513-5280.
