Dog training questions

Do you offer private dog training in St. George and Ivins?

Yes. Birdhouse Dog Co. offers private dog training in Ivins, St. George, and the surrounding Southern Utah area. Sessions take place at home, at a park, or in everyday settings where you need help. Share your location when booking so we can confirm travel arrangements.

How much does dog training cost?

Our training packages have set prices. During your consult, we meet your dog, discuss your goals, and recommend the best-fit package. We’ll walk you through what’s included and what it costs, with no commitment to book. Request a training consult to get started.

What can private dog training help us work on?

Birdhouse works with dog owners on leash pulling, barking or lunging at other dogs, recall, jumping, counter surfing, door manners, settling, crate and separation comfort, and handling for grooming or vet visits. The consultation helps identify your dog’s needs and whether the available training is a suitable fit.

Does Birdhouse offer puppy training in Southern Utah?

Yes. Birdhouse Dog Co. offers private puppy coaching focused on foundations and everyday life with your dog. Puppy coaching includes two extra sessions. Tell us your puppy’s age, vaccine status, and the habits you want help with during the consult so we can plan the sessions.

What is the difference between private coaching and boot camp?

With private coaching, you take part in one-on-one sessions, weekly or every other week, and practice at home between lessons. Boot camp is a five-week minimum stay-and-train program with daily training, followed by six follow-up sessions. We recommend a format after meeting your dog and hearing your goals.

What is included in the stay-and-train boot camp?

Birdhouse’s boot camp includes a five-week minimum stay with daily training, six follow-up sessions, two professional grooms, training supplies, and lifetime support. You receive daily photo and video updates Monday through Saturday. The consultation determines whether the program fits your dog.

Can you help my dog become more comfortable with grooming?

Handling practice for grooming is one of Birdhouse’s training topics. Because Birdhouse offers both services, you can discuss your dog’s grooming concerns alongside a training plan. Grooming appointments and training can be requested together; boot camp includes two professional grooms.

Do you travel to Santa Clara, Washington, or Hurricane for dog training?

Yes. Birdhouse offers training across Southern Utah, including Santa Clara, Washington, and Hurricane, as well as Ivins and St. George. Tell us your address and preferred setting when you reach out so we can confirm arrangements for the consult and lessons.
